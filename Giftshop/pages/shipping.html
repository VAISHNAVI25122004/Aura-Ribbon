Delivery & Logistics

Shipping Information

Every gift from Aura & Ribbon is packed with utmost care, hand-finished with signature presentation, and delivered safely to your recipient's hands.

Delivery Overview

How Your Order Arrives

Delivery Areas

We deliver nationwide across the contiguous territory, including residential addresses, business offices, and event venues. Select regional locations and international destinations are serviced via our trusted courier network.

Order Processing

Standard curated gift boxes are typically prepared and assembled within 1–2 business days. Customized or personalized gifts may require additional studio crafting time before dispatch.

Standard Delivery

Reliable ground transit with signature delivery confirmation. Ideal for birthdays, anniversaries, and planned celebrations. Estimated transit time is displayed at checkout based on destination.

Express Delivery

Priority courier service for last-minute celebrations and expedited deliveries. Orders placed before our daily studio cutoff receive expedited processing and dedicated transit.

Order Tracking

Once your parcel is sealed and leaves our studio, you will receive an email confirmation containing real-time courier tracking details and milestone alerts.

Delayed Deliveries

Weather conditions or seasonal courier surges may occasionally cause transit delays. If your parcel is delayed beyond the estimated window, our concierge team will proactively follow up.

Address Changes

Need to adjust the recipient address? Please contact us as soon as possible. We can readily modify the shipping address before your parcel is handed over to the courier.

International Shipping

We accommodate international gift orders to select worldwide destinations. Import duties, taxes, and local customs inspection timelines may apply depending on the destination country.
